ABOUT ME

Originally from New Caledonia and now proudly based on the Gold Coast, Australia, I have built my career across procurement, logistics, supply chain and operations environments while working with suppliers, freight forwarders, shipping lines and stakeholders across international markets.

Early in my career, I had the opportunity to complete a professional internship in Melbourne, Australia. This experience provided valuable exposure to international business practices and multicultural working environments, reinforcing my interest in global supply chains and international operations.

Over the years, I have worked across marine, construction, retail and freight forwarding industries throughout Australia, New Caledonia and the wider APAC region. My professional journey has taken me from freight forwarding and import/export operations to procurement, purchasing and operational coordination roles, giving me exposure to a wide range of industries, stakeholders and supply chain environments.

Working in multicultural and fast-paced environments has strengthened my adaptability, communication and coordination skills. Whether coordinating suppliers, supporting operational teams or navigating logistics challenges, I enjoy bringing people together and finding practical solutions that keep businesses moving.

What motivates me most is the human side of operations: building trusted relationships, supporting teams and creating positive working partnerships while contributing to efficient business outcomes.

I believe that strong relationships, curiosity and a willingness to keep learning are often the key ingredients behind successful operations.
